# StefanOOEOrg

Open-source tools around personal AI agents, health and sports data, and local-first apps – built by a small **AI development team** under a real Scrum process.

| Role | Account |
|---|---|
| Product owner & architect | [@StefanOOE](https://github.com/StefanOOE) |
| Lead (refines, reviews, merges) | `StefanOOELeadBot` |
| Developer (implements, opens PRs) | `StefanOOEDevBot` |

Every change goes through a task issue, a pull request, CI gates, a Lead review and the product owner's approval. Read how it works – with diagrams of the internal flow and of external contributions – in [PROCESS.md](https://github.com/StefanOOEOrg/.github/blob/main/PROCESS.md).

**Highlights:** [newsT](https://github.com/StefanOOEOrg/newsT) · [routeproof](https://github.com/StefanOOEOrg/routeproof) · [flux-workbench](https://github.com/StefanOOEOrg/flux-workbench) · MCP servers for [Garmin](https://github.com/StefanOOEOrg/mcp-garmin), [Eight Sleep](https://github.com/StefanOOEOrg/mcp-eightsleep), [public transport](https://github.com/StefanOOEOrg/mcp-scotty) and more.

Contributions welcome – all public repositories are MIT licensed.
